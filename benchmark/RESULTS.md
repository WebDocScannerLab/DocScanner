# Scan Comparison Summary

**Inputs:** 10 unchanged SmartDoc 2015 frames  
**Products shown:** [Web Document Scanner](https://web-doc-scanner.app), Adobe Scan, CamScanner, and iScanner

The [side-by-side comparison page](./COMPARISON.md) presents every source photo
and available output in a consistent layout.

## Coverage

- **[Web Document Scanner](https://web-doc-scanner.app):** 10/10 outputs available.
- **Adobe Scan:** 10/10 outputs available.
- **CamScanner:** 10/10 outputs available.
- **iScanner:** 9/10 outputs available; sample 10 is pending.

iScanner sample 10 is marked as pending. The legacy OnlineScanner files still
present in the repository are not part of this four-product comparison.

## Performance summary

The available outputs show [Web Document Scanner](https://web-doc-scanner.app) delivering a strong overall
balance across the main visual criteria:

- clean separation of the page from the surrounding background;
- effective correction of angled document geometry;
- even page backgrounds under difficult lighting;
- consistent presentation across datasheets, letters, and magazine pages.

Adobe Scan, CamScanner, and iScanner produce different trade-offs in contrast,
fine-detail retention, crop boundaries, and enhancement style. [Web Document Scanner](https://web-doc-scanner.app)'s most visible advantage in this set is the consistency of its
flattening and cleanup across changing capture angles.

## Test scope

- The set contains one source background and three document layouts.
- It does not cover receipts, handwriting, folds, or curved book pages.
- The comparison focuses on visible output characteristics rather than a
  composite numerical score.
- Results may change with app versions, devices, filters, and manual crop
  adjustments.
