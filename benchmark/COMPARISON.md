# Scan Result Comparison

**[English](./COMPARISON.md) · [中文](./COMPARISON.zh-CN.md)**

The images below show the same source photos processed by four selected
document scanners. Original photos and outputs are presented side by side for
direct visual comparison.

## Results at a glance

Across the available results, Web Document Scanner stands out for the balance
of page flattening, background cleanup, and visual consistency. Its advantage
is most visible on strongly angled photos and pages affected by uneven light,
where the output is generally cleaner and more uniform while retaining the
document structure.

Adobe Scan, CamScanner, and iScanner also produce usable results, with
differences in contrast, detail retention, crop boundaries, and enhancement
style from image to image. Results can vary by app version, device, selected
filter, crop adjustments, and source-photo quality. iScanner sample 10 is
pending and is marked accordingly.

## Test notes

- This comparison covers 10 source photos from the SmartDoc dataset.
- The set contains three document layouts from one SmartDoc background scene.
- The page focuses on visible page geometry, background cleanup, readability,
  and consistency rather than a single composite score.
- Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. This project
  is not affiliated with or endorsed by the compared products.
